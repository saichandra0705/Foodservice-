About Us

A Premium Cloud Kitchen Born from Passion for Authentic Telangana Flavours

Mint & Masala is a premier cloud kitchen nestled in Hyderabad, specializing in delivering the bold, aromatic mastery of Hyderabadi-Telangana cuisine. From fragrant ghee-infused biryanis and fiery mutton curries to chef's specials like Chama Gadda Mutton and wholesome veg delights, we bring Telangana's culinary heritage straight to your doorstep.

With an unwavering commitment to purity — using only fresh ghee, no reused oils, no food colors, or synthetic ingredients — and operations from our pristine green kitchen, every meal is cooked just like at home: healthy, natural, and with extra care for hygiene.

Whether you're craving a solo a la carte biryani or hosting an intimate family gathering, Mint & Masala guarantees an extraordinary feast with fast, reliable deliveries across Hyderabad.
